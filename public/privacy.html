Policies

Privacy policy

This policy explains what information NeonDrive ("we") collects when you visit this website or place an order, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Details you give at checkout: name, email, phone number, and shipping and billing address.
  • Order details, such as the products and quantities you buy.
  • Your cart, which is saved only in your own browser so it stays there between visits.
  • Basic technical data our hosting provider records automatically, such as IP address and browser type, used to keep the site running and secure.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. You enter card and wallet details on Stripe's secure checkout page; they are never sent to or stored by us. See the Stripe Privacy Policy.

How we use your information

To process, ship and support your order, send order-related messages, prevent fraud, and meet legal, tax and accounting obligations. We do not send marketing emails unless you ask to receive them.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising. We share it only with service providers that help us run the store, such as our payment processor (Stripe), hosting provider (Vercel), suppliers and shipping carriers, and only as needed to fulfill your order, or when required by law.

Cookies

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your cart is stored in your browser's local storage. Stripe may use cookies on its checkout page for security and fraud prevention.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed to fulfill orders, handle returns and meet legal and tax requirements.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information by emailing . Depending on where you live (for example California), you may have additional rights under local privacy laws, and we will honor valid requests. We will not treat you differently for making a request.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always on this page.
