Privacy policy

This policy explains what information LED Interior Kit collects when you use this website and place an order, and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Contact and shipping details you enter at checkout, such as your name, email, phone number and address.
  • Order details, such as the products and quantities you buy.
  • Your cart contents, which are saved in your own browser so they stay there between visits.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card and wallet details are entered on Stripe's secure checkout page and are never sent to or stored by us. Stripe's use of your information is described in the Stripe Privacy Policy.

How we use your information

We use your information to process and ship your order, send order updates, answer your questions and meet legal and tax obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We share information only with the services needed to run the store, such as our payment processor, hosting provider and shipping carriers.
