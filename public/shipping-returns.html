Shipping & returns

Shipping

We currently ship to US addresses only. Shipping is free on all orders. Delivery times are estimates and start once your order ships.

If we can't ship your order within the time stated above, we'll email you before that time with a new estimated ship date. You can then choose to cancel your order for a full refund, or wait.

Returns

  • You have 30 days from delivery to start a return.
  • Items must be unused and in their original packaging with all parts included.
  • To start a return, email with your order number. We'll reply with the return address.
  • You pay the return shipping cost unless the item arrived damaged, defective or wrong.

Refunds

Once we receive and check your return, we'll refund the item price to your original payment method. Your bank may take a few business days to show the refund.

Damaged, defective or wrong items

If your order arrives damaged, defective or wrong, email within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We'll send a replacement or a full refund, and you won't pay any shipping.

Cancellations

To cancel an order that hasn't shipped yet, email us as soon as possible. If it hasn't shipped, we'll cancel it and refund you in full.
